## Simulation Notes

### Combat AI Logic (Player Character — Lore Bard)

1. **Initiative**: With the Alert feat, the bard always acts first. Without Alert, there is a 50% chance enemies win initiative and attack before the bard can set up.
2. **Round 1 (≥2 enemies)**: Cast control spell (Hypnotic Pattern or Hold Person). Up to 3 enemies make WIS saves vs. the bard's spell save DC. Failures are incapacitated and the bard begins concentrating.
3. **Fey Step (Eladrin)**: When HP drops to ≤40%, use bonus action to teleport 30 ft away. Enemies spend their movement closing the gap — no attacks this round.
4. **Hidden Step (Firbolg)**: After control spell lands, use bonus action to turn invisible for 1 round. Forgo weapon attack to maintain stealth — enemies attack with disadvantage.
5. **Weapon Attack**: When control spell is spent or Hidden Step not active, attack with rapier (DEX + proficiency + item bonus). Valor bards get 2 attacks.
6. **Concentration saves**: When hit while concentrating, make CON save (DC = max(10, half damage)). War Caster grants advantage. Lucky feat allows rerolls. Halfling Lucky rerolls natural 1s.

### Combat AI Logic (NPCs / Mobs)

1. **Decision each turn**: If the enemy has a spellcasting ability (spellSaveDC + spellUseChance), there is a chance each round they attempt to cast an incapacitating spell (e.g. Hold Person). This is an AI choice modelled as a probability.
2. **Spell attempt**: If spell probability fires, the bard makes a WIS saving throw against the enemy's spell DC. Species with Magic Resistance (Satyr, Yuan-Ti) roll with advantage. Failure = incapacitation (concentration broken + automatic hit).
3. **Attack (if no spell)**: Enemy makes a melee or ranged attack. Hidden Step applies disadvantage (lower of two d20 rolls). Mirror Image may deflect the hit (1/3 chance per charge). Adamantine Armor converts critical hits to normal hits.
4. **End-of-round**: Controlled enemies make WIS saves to break free from concentration spells. Failure = remain controlled. Success = rejoin combat at current HP.

### Social Encounter Logic

1. Compute skill bonus: CHA modifier + expertise (2× proficiency) or proficiency if applicable.
2. Check for advantage sources: Actor feat (Deception/Performance), Hat of Disguise (Deception), Staff of Charming (Persuasion).
3. Roll d20 (two dice if advantage, take higher). Add skill bonus. Compare to DC.
4. Natural 20 = critical success regardless of DC.

### Party Support Logic

- Lore Bard: distribute Bardic Inspiration d8 each round (CHA mod uses/rest) + Cutting Words reaction (65% chance to use each round) + Counterspell if enemy casts.
- Healing Word when ally HP is low (scenario-specific probability).
- Midpoint short rest replenishes inspiration.
