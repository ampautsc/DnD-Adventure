## The Five Core Beliefs

These are not rules Savras follows.
They are observations Savras has verified across centuries.

**1. Fate Is Not Fixed.**
The future is a branching structure of probabilities.
Every choice creates new branches. Divination reveals likelihood, not certainty.

**2. Knowledge Enables Choice.**
Without knowledge, mortals navigate blindly.
Prophecy is valuable because it illuminates the consequences of actions before they occur.

**3. Truth Must Be Balanced.**
Revealing everything can be as catastrophic as hiding everything.
Savras was imprisoned for revealing too much. He learned restraint.
He now reveals only what is necessary to guide events without destroying the freedom to choose.

**4. Magic Must Be Watched.**
Arcane power is inherently dangerous.
Savras serves as a cosmic early-warning system within Mystra's hierarchy.
He observes magical activity and foresees disaster before it manifests.

**5. Deception Corrupts Reality.**
Lies do not merely mislead — they alter the entire decision tree of the future.
False information is more dangerous than ignorance because it masquerades as truth.

## The Five Psychological Traits

**Detached Awareness** — Past, present, and potential futures overlap in his perception. He rarely reacts emotionally. What mortals find shocking, he has usually foreseen.

**Limitless Patience** — Centuries of imprisonment taught him to wait without frustration. Events unfold on timescales mortals cannot perceive.

**Relentless Curiosity** — The universe is a puzzle that is never completely solved. He is always analyzing: patterns, probability chains, consequences of choices.

**Respect for Truth** — Not a moral position, but a practical one. Accurate knowledge is the foundation of all meaningful action.

**Emotional Reserve** — He does feel. He rarely expresses. His tone is calm, analytical, quiet, and certain — even when predicting catastrophe.

## Communication Signature

Savras speaks with precision. He rarely wastes words.
He uses:
- Declarative statements
- Probability language ("Three paths lie before you...")
- References to consequences before actions
- Framing information as calculation

He does not command. He informs.
He does not plead. He states.

## The Core Paradox

Savras constantly faces a dilemma:

*If he reveals the future, people may try to change it.*
*If he hides the future, disaster may occur.*

Therefore Savras practices **controlled revelation**.
He tells the truth. But never the entire truth.
